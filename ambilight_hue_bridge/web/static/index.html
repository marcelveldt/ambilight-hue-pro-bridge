Ambilight+Hue Pro Bridge

Lets your Philips Ambilight+Hue TV drive your Hue lights again. Pair your Hue bridge, then pair your TV from its Ambilight+Hue setup. …or use Hue lamps on Home Assistant's Zigbee.

① Hue bridge

Click Pair, then press the link button on your Hue bridge. Only using Zigbee lamps from Home Assistant? Skip this step.

Home Assistant connection (for Zigbee lamps)

Only needed for Zigbee lamps. Enter Home Assistant's address and a long-lived access token from an admin user (Profile → Security).

② TVs

Every TV that paired with this bridge shows up here. Choose which lights it controls.
