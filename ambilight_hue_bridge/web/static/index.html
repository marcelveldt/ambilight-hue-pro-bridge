This service poses as a Philips Hue bridge so your TV's Ambilight+Hue can connect to it. Pair your real Hue bridge below, then give each TV an entertainment area to control — the TV arranges those lights around its screen and we forward the colours to your bridge. With Home Assistant's ZHA on a Ziggurat radio, a TV can instead stream straight to Zigbee lamps.

① Your Hue bridge

The real Hue bridge your lights live on — where we send the TV's colours. Press its link button, then Pair (or Discover it on the network).

② TVs & their lights

Each paired TV controls one entertainment area from your Hue bridge — just the set of lights it drives (positions set in the Hue app are ignored; the TV places them itself). Tick split gradient zones to expose each zone of a gradient strip as its own light. Enable or disable forwarding per TV and use priority to decide which TV gets the single Hue streaming slot (a higher number wins). When Home Assistant's ZHA runs on a Ziggurat radio, a TV can stream to Zigbee lights instead: up to 10 Hue colour lamps, driven directly over Zigbee without a Hue bridge and restored to their previous state when streaming stops.
